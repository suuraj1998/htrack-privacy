Privacy Policy for Htrack

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Developer: Suraj Prasad Gouda

Information you enter and on-device storage

Htrack lets you keep a personal health journal. Information you choose to enter may include headache and blood-pressure records; sleep, steps, weight, temperature, pulse, oxygen and glucose readings; medication, symptom, mood, fall, menstrual-cycle and pregnancy notes; and family-profile names and relationships. Your selected marketplace preference is also stored on your device.

On Android, journal records are stored in an encrypted database on your device. The encryption key is generated on the device and stored using Android secure storage. Htrack does not upload or sync these journal records to an Htrack-operated server. Htrack does not require an Htrack account.

Health assistant and reports

The health assistant uses information stored on your device; journal entries are not sent to an external AI service. Reports are generated on your device. If you choose to print, save, email or share a report, the receiving printer, app, service or person may receive the health information included in that report and may handle it under their own privacy practices.

Google Play subscriptions

If you view, buy or restore Htrack Plus, Google Play processes the transaction. Htrack checks subscription product and purchase status through Google Play and stores the resulting access status and last-check time in secure storage on your device. Htrack does not receive your payment-card details. Google handles account and payment information under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Marketplace links

If you tap a product-browsing link, Htrack opens an Amazon marketplace search using a generic product category and the marketplace country you selected. Htrack does not send your journal entries or health-profile information in that search. Amazon receives the search request and may process information such as network or device details under its own privacy policy.

Advertising, analytics and sharing

Htrack does not include advertising or analytics services. Htrack does not sell your health-journal information or transmit it to Htrack servers. The third-party interactions described above are Google Play subscription processing, marketplace searches that you choose to open, and reports that you choose to export or share.

Retention and deletion

Your journal stays in the app's local storage until you delete records or remove the app's data. Removing a family profile deletes that profile's local records. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes local app data; it cannot delete copies of reports you already shared or information retained by Google Play or another recipient. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play.

Security

The Android journal database is encrypted, and its key is held in Android secure storage. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and take care when exporting reports. If the device's secure-storage key is lost, Htrack cannot recover the encrypted journal.
